# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Times

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days domestically, and 7 to 14
business days internationally. Expedited shipping, when selected at checkout,
takes 1 to 2 business days domestically.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed once an order ships. Tracking status can also be
looked up by order number.

## Lost or Delayed Packages

If a package shows as delivered but the customer has not received it, or has
not moved for more than 5 business days, this should be escalated to a human
agent to open a carrier investigation.
