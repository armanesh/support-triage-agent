# Refund Policy

## Standard Refund Window

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the purchase date.
Between 31 and 60 days, a 50% partial refund applies. After 60 days, purchases
are not eligible for a refund under standard policy.

## How Refunds Are Issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10
business days, depending on the customer's bank or card issuer.

## Exceptions

Defective or damaged items are eligible for a full refund or replacement
regardless of the standard window, provided the customer reports the issue
within 14 days of noticing the defect.
