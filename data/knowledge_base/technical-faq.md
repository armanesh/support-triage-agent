# Technical FAQ

## App Crashes

If the app crashes on launch, customers should try force-closing the app,
restarting their device, and reinstalling the latest version. Most crash
reports are resolved by updating to the current app version.

## Sync Errors

An error saying data failed to sync usually means the device lost network
connection mid-sync. Reopening the app on a stable connection typically
resolves it automatically within a few minutes.

## Reporting a Bug

Bugs that persist after a reinstall should be escalated to a human agent with
the customer's device model, app version, and steps to reproduce.
