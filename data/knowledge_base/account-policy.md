# Account Policy

## Password Resets

Customers who are locked out of their account can reset their password
through the "Forgot password" link on the login page. A reset link is sent to
the email address on file and expires after 60 minutes.

## Changing an Email Address

An account's email address can be changed from account settings once the
customer is logged in. If the customer no longer has access to the old email
and cannot log in, identity must be verified before an agent changes it
manually.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Customers who are locked out due to a lost two-factor authentication device
should contact support with proof of identity; 2FA cannot be disabled through
self-service for security reasons.
